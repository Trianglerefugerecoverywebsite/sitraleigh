  To me the Buddha was the ultimate teacher. He knew exactly what the student
  sitting in front of him needed. For those of us who wouldn't get to hear him
  in person, he left us clear instructions for meditating and dealing with the
  suffering that plagues all sentient beings. No matter how many times I read or
  listen, discuss or teach about his work, each time I am as amazed as the last.
  The reason I am so jazzed even after decades of practice is simple - it works,
  it is beautiful, and my life has changed so much just by practicing with his
  discovery.
